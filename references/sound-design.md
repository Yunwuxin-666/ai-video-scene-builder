# Sound Design

## Default Rule

For emotional or narrative scenes, decide the sound plan before generating visuals.

Not necessarily final audio generation first, but sound logic first.

## Why Sound Comes Early

Sound determines:

- pauses
- timing of reactions
- emotional weight of silence
- where the scene turns

If sound is undecided, the storyboard often becomes too visual and too literal.

## Three Layers

### 1. Environment bed
- room tone
- street noise
- store hum
- wind

### 2. Action details
- footsteps
- fabric movement
- hand movement
- phone screen or object interaction

### 3. Narrative trigger
- a name being called
- a line arriving off-screen
- a door opening
- a sudden stop in sound

## When Not To Add Music Early

Do not rush into music when the scene relies on:

- emptiness
- waiting
- tension
- delayed reaction
- realism

In those scenes, sound design and silence usually matter more than melody.
