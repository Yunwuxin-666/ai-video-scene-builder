# Workflow

## Goal

Turn one video idea into a reusable execution package instead of stopping at loose inspiration.

## Standard Sequence

1. Compress the idea into one scene sentence
2. State the dramatic purpose
3. Write the emotional arc
4. Estimate duration
5. Design sound first
6. Build the storyboard
7. Check continuity
8. Create the material checklist
9. Write shot prompts
10. Generate optional diagrams

## Why This Order

- Sound affects rhythm, pauses, and reaction timing
- Storyboard must follow emotion, not random visuals
- Material prep depends on scene logic, not the other way around
- Prompts should be the last translation layer, not the first

## First Questions To Resolve

- What is the scene really about?
- What changes emotionally from beginning to end?
- What must the audience know first, and what should arrive later?
- Is the scene driven by action, reaction, or emotional waiting?
- What is the lowest emotional point, and what changes after it?

## Common Failure Pattern

Many AI video attempts fail because the order is reversed:

- prompts first
- random image generation
- then trying to “patch” a story afterward

The result is usually pretty fragments without a real scene.
