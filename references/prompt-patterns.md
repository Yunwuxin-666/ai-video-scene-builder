# Prompt Patterns

Prompts should be organized by shot function, not by model trivia.

## Common Shot Functions

### Establishing shot
- establish space
- establish distance
- establish isolation or crowding

### State shot
- show body posture
- show waiting, hesitation, readiness, stillness

### Emotional close shot
- focus on eyes, face, breath, hesitation

### Detail shot
- use hand, fabric, object edge, foot movement, or small face detail

### Reaction shot
- a pause before turning
- a delayed recognition
- a moment of disbelief

### Turn shot
- gradual turn
- body reacts before full face reveal

### Closing shot
- restore or change the spatial relationship
- show what is different from the opening shot

## Prompt Rule

For each prompt, keep these layers visible:

- shot type
- scene anchor
- subject action
- emotional intention
- lighting / atmosphere
- what the viewer should notice first
