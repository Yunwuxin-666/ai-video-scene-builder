# Apple-Style Commercials

Use this reference when the user asks for 苹果风格, iPhone-style, Apple Watch-style, Mac/iPad-style, or a restrained Apple-like commercial.

Do not copy Apple logos, exact UI, campaign taglines, protected shots, or product identities unless the user is analyzing references. Borrow the mechanism and visual grammar.

## Core Apple Mechanism

Apple-style advertising usually sells the feeling after a product quietly solves a human problem.

Use this chain:

`ordinary world -> precise inconvenience -> elegant tool action -> visible result -> restrained line`

## Apple Rules

- Start with life, not product.
- Make the problem concrete enough to film in one frame.
- Let the tool action feel lighter than the problem.
- Keep one feature spine.
- Use objects and actions to cut, not explanatory narration.
- Prefer dry humor or quiet relief over big emotion.
- Use ordinary places: farm, library, kitchen, school play, commute, office, street corner, family table.
- Keep typography sparse and end cards short.
- Let sound do real work: ping, click, page turn, shutter, notification, footstep, silence.

## Rhythm For 60 Seconds

| Segment | Time | Function |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 0-5s | Show an odd but readable situation. |
| Problem build | 5-18s | Make the inconvenience feel real. |
| Product enters | 18-28s | The tool appears because the story needs it. |
| Action chain | 28-45s | One feature is proven through movement, sound, or montage. |
| Result | 45-55s | Human outcome lands. |
| End card | 55-60s | One short line, no explanation. |

## Apple-Like Visual Traits

- Clean but lived-in spaces.
- Soft natural light or simple practical light.
- Strong single object: phone, watch, book, key, cup, backpack, bike, door.
- Clear hand action.
- Matched cuts: tap -> knock, swipe -> slide, turn page -> open door, ping -> head turn.
- Product appears in hand or screen POV, not floating in a sci-fi void.

## Apple-Style Failure Modes

- Too much logo.
- Too many features.
- Product appears before the problem.
- Generic beautiful lifestyle shots with no conflict.
- Fake futuristic UI.
- Slogan explains what the scene already proved.
- Every shot is clean but nothing is happening.

## Example Mechanism Templates

### Find

Someone loses a small thing in a huge place. The product makes one quiet sound or signal. The huge place becomes searchable.

### Fit

A group or memory cannot fit into the frame. One camera action reveals the full relationship.

### Last

A day keeps stretching beyond expectation. The product is still alive when the person needs it.

### Protect

An everyday accident looks fatal for the device. The device survives with comic understatement.

### Remember

A person has a fragment of memory. The product connects fragments into one recoverable trail.
