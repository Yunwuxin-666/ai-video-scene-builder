# Commercial Film Settings

Use this reference when the user asks for 广告片, 商业短片, 产品短片, 品牌片, 苹果风格短片, or wants to add advertising elements to an AI video prompt package.

## Add This Section

Place this after `〖基础设定〗`:

```markdown
## 〖广告片设定〗

目标观众：
产品/工具角色：
核心功能：
用户原本的麻烦：
功能翻译成的生活动作：
情绪收益：
证明动作：
产品第一次出现的时机：
产品露出尺度：
片尾收束：
不能做成什么：
```

## How To Fill It

- **目标观众**: name the real viewer, not a demographic label only. Example: "经常在碎片时间记录想法的创作者".
- **产品/工具角色**: define the product as a helper in the scene, not a hero. Example: "安静整理线索的工具".
- **核心功能**: pick one spine. Do not list five features.
- **用户原本的麻烦**: make it visible. If it cannot be filmed, rewrite it.
- **功能翻译成的生活动作**: turn a feature into something the actor does. Search becomes "tap one clue"; durability becomes "survive a comic accident"; privacy becomes "stop invisible followers".
- **情绪收益**: describe the feeling after the problem is solved: relaxed, in control, connected, free, less embarrassed.
- **证明动作**: choose one shot that proves the claim without explaining it.
- **产品第一次出现的时机**: delay until the problem needs it.
- **产品露出尺度**: decide whether the product is hidden, partial, used in hand, screen POV, or end-card packshot.
- **片尾收束**: one short line; no slogan pile.
- **不能做成什么**: define failure modes: tech demo, corporate promo, fake lifestyle montage, over-explained tutorial.

## Advertising Mechanisms

### Big Problem, Small Action

Make the problem visually large and the product action small. The contrast creates the ad.

### Feature As Scene

Translate:

| Feature | Scene |
|---|---|
| Search | finding a tiny thing in a huge space |
| Battery | a long day that keeps going |
| Camera | catching a moment others miss |
| Wide angle | a cramped group finally fits |
| Privacy | invisible watchers become visible |
| Durability | a comic accident becomes harmless |
| Sync | several places become one workflow |

### One Functional Spine

Do not combine too many features. If the short is about "finding", every shot should serve finding. If it is about "wide angle", every shot should serve fitting more into the frame.

### Product Timing

- 0-20%: human world and problem.
- 20-45%: product appears as a natural action.
- 45-80%: action chain proves the function.
- 80-100%: emotional result and end card.

## Commercial Negative Prompt

`不要企业宣传片口吻，不要参数堆叠，不要主持人口播，不要大字报卖点，不要电商详情页，不要悬浮科技 UI，不要无意义快切，不要全程产品硬广，不要把人物拍成工具人。`
