# Apple-style visual direction

Load only when the user explicitly requests Apple-style advertising, Apple-inspired restraint, or revision of an active Apple-style route. Do not apply these rules to general commercials, documentaries, narrative films, or product prompts.

## Distraction scenes

- Do not introduce too many clear side characters in the first 6-8 seconds after setup.
- Let disturbance first appear as shadow, voice, peripheral color, chair movement, or a partial shoulder.
- Keep the protagonist mentally active through micro-frown, eye-line flick, continued hand operation, or a brief pause followed by action.
- Avoid repeated blank staring reactions and jumpy cuts to a new visible character for every beat.

## Texture

- Use two to four material anchors per shot.
- Keep product materials as the cleanest points in frame: metal uses controlled bevel highlights, glass keeps clean reflections, and silicone, rubber, and fabric retain believable matte texture.
- Keep skin and clothing natural.
- Reject cheap plastic, waxy skin, oily highlights, dirty product edges, random scratches, and clutter unless required by the brief.

## Color

- Favor controlled neutrals and product-led accents: warm white, cool white, silver gray, black glass, rose gold, soft beige, or one screen/accent color.
- Reject dirty yellow, heavy black-gray disaster grading, oversaturated orange/blue, cyberpunk blue, alarm red, or muddy low contrast unless explicitly requested.

## Composition

- Lock the movement axis and screen geography before complex action.
- Keep protagonist, product, obstacle, and exit direction stable.
- Use foreground occlusion, compressed paths, leading lines, and shallow depth for pressure.
- Open the frame and reduce clutter after release.
- Give product end cards stable geometry and deliberate text-safe negative space.

## Lighting

- Use motivated window, station, store, practical, or studio sources.
- Create impact through clean key light, controlled rim/backlight, bounce fill, and restrained reflections.
- For epic but clean moments, prefer white mist, strong backlight, foreground blur, and readable silhouettes over fire, black smoke, dirty dust, or destruction.

## Lens and camera

- Use 35mm for human movement and spatial relation.
- Use 50mm for medium pressure and follow shots.
- Use 85-100mm for product/action inserts.
- Use a restrained 70mm-style packshot when appropriate.
- Move from handheld pressure to stable tool action, then smoother follow or lock-off after release.
- Avoid action-camera chaos, random whip pans, spin transitions, first-person shake, and unmotivated zooms.

## Per-shot check

- one story action;
- one composition role;
- one motivated light source or light logic;
- one lens/camera movement choice;
- two to four material anchors;
- restrained palette;
- continuity of hand, product, movement axis, and screen direction.

Use these as internal checks. Do not emit separate visual-rule tables unless requested.
