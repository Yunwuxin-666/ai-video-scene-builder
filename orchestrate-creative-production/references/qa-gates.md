# Quality gates

## Spoken content

- Opens from a concrete experience, scene, change, or tension.
- Has one central line of reasoning rather than a menu of generic directions.
- Uses the user's own cases as evidence.
- Contains a clear personal judgment, not only accepted industry claims.
- Removes report-like taxonomy unless it materially clarifies the argument.
- Makes abstract concepts observable through actions, contrast, or consequences.
- Ends with a sharper conclusion than "improve judgment" or "embrace AI."

## Visual IP

- Character age, proportions, face, hair, silhouette, clothing structure, palette, and signature props remain consistent.
- Approved identity is locked before action, scene, poster, or motion extension.
- New references influence only the requested dimension.
- Pose, hands, props, perspective, lighting, and material logic are plausible.
- Poster hierarchy supports the intended platform and first-view focal point.
- Character-specific traits have not leaked into global style rules.

## Film and AI video

- The active route has one premise, protagonist goal, obstacle, and ending state.
- Every beat has a cause, central visible action, and result.
- Every shot has a start state and end state; adjacent states are compatible.
- Character, clothing, props, product state, screen direction, space, time, and sound continuity remain stable.
- Every reference states what it controls and what it must not transfer.
- Conflicting references are resolved by the producer, not blended.
- One primary style, at most one secondary influence, one palette system, and one motivated light logic remain active.
- Specialist departments changed only their owned fields.
- The final platform prompt was compiled after story and continuity locks.
- Global locks and reference bindings are not repeated in every shot.
- Long continuous generations are used only when subject, space, action objective, lighting, and visual law remain coherent.
- Platform capability claims are current and distinguish official facts from assumptions.

## Technical or course document

- Uses Goal -> Input -> Operation -> Output -> Check when the user wants an SOP.
- Every step states the required references and expected result.
- Prompts are clean, copyable, and attached to the correct stage.
- Images appear near the step they support.
- Examples and general rules are clearly distinguished.
- Local and cloud versions do not contain broken links, encoding damage, clipping, or accidental blank pages.

## Delivery evidence

Before claiming completion, verify each named artifact exists and opens, all required stages are present, links resolve when checkable, and the final result reflects the latest locked decisions.
