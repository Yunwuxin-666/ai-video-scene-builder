# Rhythm, Camera, and Prompt Rules

## Rhythm Map

| Script rhythm | Use these actions | Camera impact |
|---|---|---|
| 短、狠、突然 | elbow, knee, shoulder check, wall shove, wrist snap | tight medium, quick push-in, short impact pause |
| 狼狈、危险、真实 | ground scramble, wrist fight, guard, roll, crawl, body pin | low handheld, shallow depth, breath and fabric sound |
| 压迫、窒息 | hallway fight, crowding, clinch, wall collision, blocked exit | 35mm tracking, foreground occlusion, compressed lines |
| 展示实力 | clean counter, kick, throw, disarm, one-against-many beat | full-body medium, stable read, precise hit point |
| 高手感、优雅 | weapon arc, foot pivot, body turn, controlled landing | smooth lateral move, wide enough to show line |
| 逃脱、追逐 | vault, slide, shoulder through gap, rail turn, obstacle dodge | wide tracking, side/rear follow, clear direction |
| 喜剧、误打误撞 | prop block, pause, accidental hit, delayed reaction | medium shot, readable object relation, reaction beat |
| 游戏感、动捕感 | readable combo, hard pose, recovery frame, silhouette line | three-quarter full-body, locked or clean orbit |
| 复仇、压抑爆发 | restrained start, sudden close hit, controlled finish | slow push, impact shake only at hit |
| 惊险、险胜 | near miss, stumble, desperate counter, unstable recovery | off-balance handheld, foreground threat passing lens |

## Camera Impact Rules

- Use 24mm only when space and movement matter: chase, corridor, group fight, weapon arc.
- Use 35mm for most human action: body path, medium pressure, readable footwork.
- Use 50mm for close striking, grappling pressure, emotional compression.
- Use 85-100mm for inserts: hand grab, knife line, weapon clash, foot plant, object break.
- Do not overuse shaky camera. Impact comes from readable body mechanics plus a timed hit emphasis.
- Place the camera on the action line before writing the prompt: side, three-quarter front, over-shoulder, low angle, or ground-level.
- For multi-person fights, keep enemies entering the threat zone one by one.
- For weapons, make the weapon line visible but do not let it dominate every frame.
- For AI video, avoid complex readable text, precise logos, and too many simultaneous contacts.

## Prompt Formula

Use this order for copy-ready prompts:

1. stable subject and continuity details;
2. location, time, spatial anchors;
3. one visible action chain with setup, force, reaction, recovery;
4. camera: shot size, lens feel, angle, support, movement;
5. lighting, color, material texture, mood;
6. sound cues;
7. style and safety constraints;
8. negative prompt.

## Universal Negative Prompt

禁止动作断裂、人物漂浮、无重力飞行、肢体变形、手指错误、关节扭曲、武器穿模、武器突然消失、衣服突然变化、场景跳变、人物位置混乱、多人同时乱打、随机镜头旋转、过度运动模糊、看不清关键动作、血腥特写、内脏或断肢、过度慢动作、廉价游戏特效、夸张火花、无原因爆炸。

## Output Compact Example

动作链 should sound like this:

主角左脚后撤半步避开直拳，右手压住对方手腕，左肩贴近对方胸口切入内线，右脚卡住对方前脚外侧，转胯用肩撞破坏重心；对方后背撞到墙面，持刀手被迫抬高，主角立刻收回右手护住下巴，半侧身恢复防守。

Prompt should not sound like:

主角和敌人激烈搏斗，动作很帅，镜头震撼。
