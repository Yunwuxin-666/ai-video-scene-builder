# Scene Structure

## Core Principle

A scene is not just a location and a character. A scene becomes usable when it has:

- a clear situation
- a dramatic purpose
- an emotional line
- an information order

## Minimum Scene Breakdown

When planning a scene, define:

1. Scene sentence
2. Dramatic purpose
3. Emotional arc
4. Time and space framing
5. Key beat changes
6. Ending state

## Useful Questions

- What is the character trying to get in this scene?
- What is stopping them?
- What does the audience know at the start?
- What should the audience feel by the end?
- What changes between the opening and the last shot?

## Emotional Arc Format

Keep it simple and directional:

- expectation
- uncertainty
- disappointment
- lowest point
- reversal
- release

Not every scene needs all six, but every scene should move.

## Information Order

Do not dump all information at once. Decide:

- what is established through space
- what is established through behavior
- what is established through expression
- what is withheld until later

This is where the scene starts to feel cinematic instead of descriptive.
