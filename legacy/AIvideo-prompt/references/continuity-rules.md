# Continuity Rules

AI video breaks most often at the scene-connection level, not the single-shot level.

## Check These First

- character identity continuity
- scene continuity
- screen direction continuity
- eyeline continuity
- action continuity
- emotional continuity

## Simple Continuity Checks

### Character
- Is it obviously the same person?
- Are hairstyle, clothes, and silhouette stable enough?

### Space
- Does the place still feel like the same location?
- Has lighting or layout drifted too far?

### Axes and direction
- Did the character suddenly flip direction?
- Did left/right positions change without motivation?

### Eyeline
- If someone looks off-screen in one shot, does the next shot match that relation?

### Action
- Does the next shot catch the same movement at a believable moment?

### Emotion
- Does the face and body state continue from the previous shot?

## Practical Rule

Do not approve a multi-shot scene just because each shot looks good alone.
Approve it only if the audience will not get lost after the cut.
