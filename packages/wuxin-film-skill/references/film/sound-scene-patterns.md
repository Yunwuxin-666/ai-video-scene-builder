# Sound Scene Patterns

Use this file when a user asks for scene-based sound combinations or needs fast presets.

## Suspense / Corridor / Unknown Threat

- Environment: HVAC low hum, fluorescent buzz, distant drip, room tone, far metal door.
- Foley: slow footsteps with reverb, fabric friction, breath close to mic, hand touching wall or handle.
- Emotional layer: sub drone, reverse swell, faint tinnitus, air pressure drop.
- BGM: atonal strings, low drone, sparse piano single notes, no clear melody.
- Avoid: loud jump-scare too early, horror trailer braam, overused heartbeat throughout.

## Emotional Drama / Rain Night / Farewell

- Environment: rain on window or car roof, wet road pass-bys, muffled city, interior room tone.
- Foley: breath, seat leather, phone vibration, hand on glass, door closing.
- Emotional layer: soft low-frequency pressure, small silence after key line.
- BGM: low piano, cello, soft strings, slow tempo, restrained melody.
- Avoid: melodramatic pop ballad, obvious tearjerker strings, lyrics under dialogue.

## Luxury Product / High-End Commercial

- Environment: near-silent studio, controlled air, clean reflections.
- Foley: metal click, glass tap, magnetic snap, fabric/leather rub, button press, precision slide.
- Emotional layer: sub hit, tight whoosh, clean riser, short impact.
- BGM: minimal electronic, deep bass, precise pulse, cold synth, sparse orchestral accent.
- Avoid: busy corporate music, cheap swoosh library feel, loud cymbal crashes.

## AI / Technology / Interface

- Environment: quiet lab or office tone, soft electrical bed.
- Foley: UI click, data pulse, swipe, notification, screen transition, keyboard.
- Emotional layer: granular shimmer, digital riser, low startup tone.
- BGM: ambient pad, arpeggiated synth, light electronic beat, orderly growth.
- Avoid: chaotic cyberpunk unless requested, game UI sounds, over-futuristic lasers.

## Course / Education / Knowledge Clarity

- Environment: quiet studio, page turn, pen, subtle room tone, soft laptop fan.
- Foley: marker, keyboard, paper, mouse click, slide transition.
- Emotional layer: light pulse when ideas connect, gentle sound bridge between sections.
- BGM: warm piano, soft pad, subtle pulse, 80-105 BPM, low melody density.
- Avoid: heroic corporate drums, inspirational stock music, music that competes with speech.

## Sports / Training / Montage

- Environment: gym tone, outdoor air, court squeak, crowd tail if needed.
- Foley: breath, foot friction, impact, equipment, cloth, body movement, sweat drop.
- Emotional layer: heartbeat, sub hit, riser, rhythmic impacts.
- BGM: drums, bass, electronic pulse, string ostinato, rising tempo or density.
- Avoid: generic trailer music if the scene is intimate, constant maximum intensity.

## Horror / Jump Scare / Haunted Room

- Environment: old wood, pipe knock, wind leak, distant room tone, electrical instability.
- Foley: floor creak, door hinge, breath, hand tremor, cloth.
- Emotional layer: reverse inhale, sub drop, tinnitus, sudden silence, metal scrape.
- BGM: drone, atonal strings, prepared piano, fractured textures.
- Avoid: cheap scream stingers, cartoon monster sounds, music revealing the scare too early.

## Documentary / Nature

- Environment: wind, birds, water, grass, insects, distant animal, natural air.
- Foley: footsteps on soil, equipment touch only if observational.
- Emotional layer: usually minimal; use silence and natural detail.
- BGM: long strings, piano, woodwinds, restrained ambient.
- Avoid: over-scoring nature, drowning ambience, fake epic scale.

## Comedy / Light Daily Life

- Environment: realistic room or street tone.
- Foley: exaggerated but believable prop sounds, chair, cup, small accidents, footsteps.
- Emotional layer: short pause before punchline, tiny sting after reaction.
- BGM: pizzicato strings, marimba, light drums, ukulele only if tone allows.
- Avoid: cartoon boings unless explicitly stylized, music explaining every joke.

## Memory / Childhood / Family

- Environment: fan, kitchenware, distant TV, summer insects, old clock, window wind.
- Foley: bowl, chopsticks, fabric, footsteps on old floor, photo album.
- Emotional layer: tape noise, soft room bloom, gentle silence.
- BGM: acoustic guitar, piano, harmonica, small strings, warm imperfect texture.
- Avoid: sentimental overkill, tragedy-coded strings unless the story demands it.
