### 夏日蓝天低机位胶片人像

Use for fresh outdoor portraits with blue-sky negative space, low-angle grass foreground, hard summer sunlight, natural props, and relaxed lifestyle performance.

- Trigger phrases: 夏日蓝天人像, 低机位胶片写真, 日系春夏户外写真, 草地蓝天少女感写真, 小红书夏日胶片人像.
- Visual grammar: low-angle upward portrait, large blue-sky negative space, grass foreground, subject on a slope or open lawn, flowers / film camera / straw hat / canvas bag as lifestyle anchors.
- Camera: 24-28mm low-angle full-body or medium-wide portrait, camera height around 30-50cm, vertical 4:5 or 3:4, foreground grass creates depth, subject placed in the lower half of the frame.
- Lighting: real afternoon hard sunlight from upper side, sky fill, hat-brim shadow preserved, hair and flower edges catch sunlight; do not flatten the face with beauty light.
- Palette: clean cyan-blue sky, fresh grass green, warm orange/yellow flowers, pale blue fabric, white cotton, small warm accessory accents.
- Materials: real skin texture, cotton/linen wrinkles, straw-hat weave, paper-wrapped flowers, canvas bag fabric, film-camera metal and leather.
- Performance: not a sweet studio smile; use a relaxed pause, looking up at clouds, slight squint in sunlight, natural body weight shift, casual hand adjustment around flowers or camera.
- Video add-on: 4-6 second locked or lightly handheld low-angle shot; grass and hair move in the wind, the subject looks up and slightly adjusts the flowers; no dramatic camera move.

- Trigger phrases: 3D IP 潮玩材质, 3D IP材质, 插画转3D效果图, 插画3D化, Blender潮玩建模, C4D潮玩建模, 3D CG潮玩材质, 收藏级哑光软胶潮玩, 哑光PVC潮玩材质, 高端盲盒手办材质, Premium Matte Vinyl Collectible Material.
- Do not trigger for: generic 3D, IP, 潮玩, 手办, 角色设计, 产品渲染, or 卡通 requests alone. This is an opt-in material card, not the default surface treatment for all 3D characters.
- Identity boundary: preserve the user's silhouette, head-to-body ratio, facial marks, anatomy, color zoning, costume structure, emblem or logo placement, tail, appendages, and proprietary details. The card may improve material response and manufacturing finish only; it must not redesign the IP.
- Modeling language: translate flat illustration shapes into clean 3D volumes with Blender / C4D-like subdivision modeling, clay-sculpted roundness, full silhouettes, soft bevels, and no razor-sharp edges. “Clay” describes the rounded modeling process, not a mandatory fingerprinted clay surface.
- Material construction: treat the figure as professionally sculpted, injection-molded, assembled, airbrush-painted, and studio rendered. Use a toy-like PBR system built from soft vinyl, semi-translucent resin, matte rotocast vinyl, soft ceramic-like coating, and frosted ABS plastic. - Primary surface: smooth rounded vinyl-resin with a creamy matte-to-satin finish, high roughness, broad diffuse highlights, soft reflection rolloff, and restrained SSS. Keep subtle micro-roughness without visible pores, clay fingerprints, ceramic cracks, or sticky rubber grain.
- Light surfaces: warm cream or neutral pale coating with slight subsurface scattering and soft milky edge transitions. The material remains mostly opaque and solid; use a soft ceramic-coated appearance without brittle porcelain glaze, crackle, or internal glow.
- Dark surfaces: graphite or soft black semi-matte PVC with medium roughness. Preserve readable form through wide restrained highlights and reflected fill; do not crush dark areas into dead black or turn them into polished lacquer.

- Conditional part mapping: skin-like areas use fine soft vinyl plus restrained semi-translucent resin; hair or large sculpted masses use frosted resin or semi-matte plastic; goggles or original transparent parts use jelly-like translucent acrylic with soft Fresnel edge reflections and light volumetric transmission; existing metal or mechanical parts use low-reflection painted metal or frosted baked-enamel shells with fine particles and subtle orange-peel texture; existing fabric or seat parts use soft knit, linen, or suede with fine fibers and gentle fold shadows. Activate only the materials supported by the original IP; never invent these parts merely to use the list.
- Roughness hierarchy: main body high-roughness matte; hair and secondary masses matte-to-satin; transparent acrylic uses controlled gloss and refraction; painted metal keeps low reflectivity; fabric remains soft and diffuse; small rigid structures may carry tighter reflections. Keep the family coherent rather than assigning every possible material to every part.
- Manufacturing finish: high-precision digital sculpt, soft mold-safe bevels, clean recesses, hidden assembly seams, invisible injection parting lines, no exposed joints, no floating pieces, and physically plausible contact shadows at every connection.
- Rendering: high-end animated-feature collectible logic with Blender / C4D / Octane-like clean CG finish, without copying any studio-specific character design. Use physically based rendering, large softbox illumination, soft ambient fill, global illumination, warm-white rim highlights, clean AO, natural contact shadows, protected pale highlights, readable dark values, and premium brand-toy clarity. Avoid generic game-character shading or over-sharpened CGI.
- Background boundary: use only a minimal warm-white, off-white, or very light gray seamless background with one faint grounding shadow. No room, landscape, architecture, furniture, decorative pattern, text, border, poster layout, prop, pet, or extra character unless the user separately requests it.
- Combination rule: this is a base material card and may be paired with one separate theme or scene card. When combined, this card owns character material and manufacturing finish; the second card owns palette, environment, composition, or narrative atmosphere. The second card must not replace the locked PVC/ABS material language unless the user asks for a different substance.
- Avoid: applying every optional material at once, inventing goggles, metal, machinery, fabric, or furniture that the IP does not contain; brittle porcelain glaze, ceramic crackle, real human skin, pores, realistic individual hair strands, clay fingerprints, wax figure, full-body gummy transparency, sticky rubber, cheap plastic, oily mirror highlights, metallic skin, internal glow, excessive SSS, obvious assembly seams, injection parting lines, exposed joints, uneven paint, brush marks, scratches, dust, weathering, low-poly game model, cheap CGI, clipped whites, dead-black shadows, floating subject, or missing contact shadow.
