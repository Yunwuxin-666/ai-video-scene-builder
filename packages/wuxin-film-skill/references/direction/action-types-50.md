# 50 Cinematic Fight and Action Classes

Use this file to select action classes from a script beat. Pick one primary class and optionally one support class. Do not cram several unrelated classes into one prompt.

Each class contains: action grammar, best scene use, camera note, and prompt seed.

## Striking and Close Combat

### 01. Boxing Pressure
- Grammar: guard up, foot step, jab/cross/hook, opponent head or shoulder reaction, guard recovery.
- Use: underground fight, police/crime realism, hard male lead, tight room.
- Camera: 35mm or 50mm medium close, shoulder-level handheld, short push-in on hit.
- Seed: 左脚前踏，左手刺拳试探，右肩转入摆拳，对手头部偏移后退半步，主角收拳回防。

### 02. Dirty Boxing Clinch
- Grammar: collar tie, short hook, forearm frame, shoulder pressure, head position control.
- Use: cramped elevator, bar fight, gritty realism, exhausted fighters.
- Camera: tight medium, bodies fill frame, breath and fabric sound.
- Seed: 主角贴近对手胸口，左前臂卡住颈侧，右手短勾拳打向肋部，对手弓身后被推到墙边。

### 03. Elbow Strike Burst
- Grammar: close distance, elbow chamber, hip turn, jaw/shoulder reaction, compact recovery.
- Use: short-hard-sudden rhythm, assassin, hallway, elevator, car door.
- Camera: 50mm close, micro impact shake.
- Seed: 主角侧身让过拳线，右肘从胸前短促横切，对手肩膀猛缩撞向门框。

### 04. Knee Strike Control
- Grammar: grab collar/neck/arm, pull down, knee rises, opponent folds, release or throw.
- Use: clinch, Muay Thai flavor, brutal but non-gory impact.
- Camera: medium close, slight low angle, impact sound deep.
- Seed: 主角双手扣住对方衣领向下拉，右膝顶向腹部位置，对手弯腰失衡。

### 05. Palm Heel / Open-Hand Strike
- Grammar: open guard, palm heel line, face/shoulder reaction, no fist injury.
- Use: professional bodyguard, restrained expert, public place.
- Camera: clean medium, less brutal, precise contact.
- Seed: 主角左手拨开对方手臂，右掌根短促推击下颌线，对手后仰退开。

### 06. Hammer Fist / Downward Smash
- Grammar: raise compact fist, downward strike to arm/weapon/shoulder, object drop.
- Use: disarm, table fight, ground scramble.
- Camera: top/side close insert, 85mm for hand impact.
- Seed: 主角扣住对方手腕，右拳从上向下砸向持刀前臂，刀柄松动。

### 07. Head Slip and Counter
- Grammar: threat line, head slips outside, weight loads, counter punch, guard reset.
- Use: skilled fighter display, boxing realism.
- Camera: side medium, show head movement clearly.
- Seed: 对手直拳贴脸掠过，主角头部向外侧滑开，重心压到前脚，右拳直线反击。

### 08. Shoulder Check
- Grammar: step inside, shoulder drives, opponent chest/back hits wall, subject stays balanced.
- Use: sudden corridor impact, crowd break, short contact.
- Camera: close 35mm, wall impact shake.
- Seed: 主角低头切入内线，右肩顶进对方胸口，把对方撞到走廊墙面。

### 09. Body Shot Collapse
- Grammar: guard distraction, rib/solar plexus hit, opponent folds, protagonist circles out.
- Use: gritty fight, professional boxing, no gore.
- Camera: medium close, sound sells hit.
- Seed: 主角左手压开对方护臂，右拳短促打向肋部，对手弓身，主角向外侧移步。

### 10. Slap / Humiliation Beat
- Grammar: visible pause, open-hand slap, head snap, silence/reaction.
- Use: character power, comedic insult, social tension before violence.
- Camera: static medium, let reaction breathe.
- Seed: 主角没有后退，只抬起右手一记清脆耳光，对手脸偏向一侧，全场停顿一拍。

## Kicks and Lower Body

### 11. Low Kick / Leg Sweep Damage
- Grammar: plant foot, hip turns, shin/instep attacks outer leg, opponent knee buckles.
- Use: realistic striking, breaking balance, clean skill display.
- Camera: full-body medium or low side angle.
- Seed: 主角左脚踩稳，髋部打开，右腿低扫对方前腿外侧，对手膝盖被扫偏。

### 12. Front Push Kick
- Grammar: guard, knee chamber, sole pushes chest/hip, opponent creates distance.
- Use: escape, spacing, doorway, crowd control.
- Camera: side medium, show push vector.
- Seed: 主角后撤避开抓手，右膝抬起，脚掌前蹬对方胸口，把人推出一步。

### 13. Side Kick Stop-Hit
- Grammar: turn hip, extend heel, stop forward rush, recover to stance.
- Use: strong female lead, hallway, clean counter.
- Camera: full-body 35mm, low angle enhances line.
- Seed: 对手冲来时，主角侧身转髋，右脚跟直线踹向躯干，对手冲势被截停。

### 14. Roundhouse Kick
- Grammar: pivot support foot, hip rotation, shin/instep arc, opponent arm/head reaction.
- Use: martial arts showcase, training hall, decisive beat.
- Camera: medium wide, preserve leg arc.
- Seed: 主角左脚旋转支撑，右腿从外侧弧线踢出，对手抬臂格挡后侧退。

### 15. Hook Kick / Crescent Line
- Grammar: leg lifts, arc passes guard, heel snaps back, controlled landing.
- Use: stylish expert, tournament, game PV.
- Camera: side full-body, slight slow hit emphasis.
- Seed: 主角右腿高位扫过对方护手，脚跟回勾擦过肩侧，落地后双手回防。

### 16. Spinning Back Kick
- Grammar: look over shoulder, pivot, heel drives backward, opponent thrown back.
- Use: high-impact single move, climax, trained fighter.
- Camera: wide enough for rotation; avoid too tight.
- Seed: 主角先看向身后目标，左脚旋转，右脚跟从背后直线踹出，对手被顶退。

### 17. Knee Slide / Low Dodge Kick
- Grammar: drop level, slide/duck, low kick/trip, stand or roll out.
- Use: chase fight, wet floor, desperate evasion.
- Camera: low tracking, ground texture visible.
- Seed: 主角低身滑过挥来的棍子，左腿顺势扫向对方脚踝，让对方踉跄。

### 18. Stomp / Pinning Foot
- Grammar: opponent down or weapon near floor, foot pins wrist/object, control not gore.
- Use: disarm, interrogation, dominance.
- Camera: insert on foot and hand, 85mm.
- Seed: 刀滑到地面时，主角右脚踩住对方手腕旁的刀柄，迫使对方停止抢夺。

## Grappling, Throws, and Control

### 19. Wrist Control / Joint Line
- Grammar: catch wrist, rotate thumb line, step off angle, opponent bends or drops weapon.
- Use: disarm, professional restraint, close quarters.
- Camera: hand insert then medium continuation.
- Seed: 主角抓住对方手腕，沿拇指方向旋转下压，同时侧步离开刀线。

### 20. Arm Drag to Back
- Grammar: parry wrist, pull across, step behind, back control or shove.
- Use: efficient trained fighter, non-lethal control.
- Camera: side 35mm, show body crossing line.
- Seed: 主角把对方右臂拖过自己胸前，身体绕到背后，双手推肩让对方撞向桌边。

### 21. Hip Throw
- Grammar: grip, hip entry, load opponent weight, turn, opponent falls over hip, follow stance.
- Use: judo flavor, decisive reversal.
- Camera: medium wide, keep feet and hip visible.
- Seed: 主角抓住衣领和手腕，右脚切入对方身前，髋部贴住对方重心，转身把人摔到地面。

### 22. Shoulder Throw
- Grammar: arm captured, back turns under arm, knees bend, opponent goes over shoulder.
- Use: classic counter, office/dojo.
- Camera: one-shot medium, low landing angle.
- Seed: 主角扣住对方右臂，转身钻到手臂下方，弯膝发力，对方从肩上翻落。

### 23. Double-Leg Takedown
- Grammar: level change, shoulder to hips, hands behind knees, drive through, floor impact.
- Use: MMA realism, survival fight.
- Camera: low front/side, follow drop.
- Seed: 主角突然降重心，肩膀顶住对方髋部，双手抱住膝后向前推进，把人摔倒。

### 24. Single-Leg Trip
- Grammar: catch one leg, lift or pull, outside trip, opponent falls sideways.
- Use: realistic messy fight, lower risk than big throw.
- Camera: medium, show leg capture.
- Seed: 主角抱住对方前腿向外拉，自己的右脚扫过支撑脚，对方侧摔到地面。

### 25. Wall Pin Control
- Grammar: redirect attack, forearm across chest/neck line, body weight pins to wall.
- Use: interrogation, hallway, bodyguard restraint.
- Camera: close side, wall fills background.
- Seed: 主角把对方手臂压到墙上，左前臂横在胸口位置，身体重量向前压住。

### 26. Table Slam / Surface Impact
- Grammar: grip shoulder/arm, rotate, guide upper body into table, object reaction.
- Use: bar, kitchen, office, messy environmental fight.
- Camera: medium with tabletop foreground.
- Seed: 主角抓住对方衣领转身，把对方肩背按向桌面，杯子和纸张被震起。

### 27. Choke Defense / Airway Escape
- Grammar: hands peel, chin tuck, step angle, elbow or wrist lever, escape to side.
- Use: danger without gore, realistic self-defense.
- Camera: close but keep hands readable.
- Seed: 对方双手掐住主角衣领逼近，主角收下巴，双手从内侧撑开手腕，侧步脱出。

### 28. Ground Guard Scramble
- Grammar: back on floor, forearm guard, knee shield, hip escape, roll or stand.
- Use: messy survival, parking lot, rain, crime realism.
- Camera: ground-level handheld.
- Seed: 主角背部贴地，用左前臂护脸，右膝顶住对方髋部，向左侧翻滚脱出压制。

### 29. Mount Escape / Bridge and Roll
- Grammar: trapped underneath, trap arm/foot, bridge hips, roll opponent, recover guard.
- Use: ground fight, desperate reversal.
- Camera: tight top/side, readable hip bridge.
- Seed: 主角被压在地面，先夹住对方一只手臂和同侧脚，再猛抬髋部翻滚，把对方掀到侧面。

### 30. Rear Control / Back Take
- Grammar: slip behind, chest to back, arms control shoulders/wrist, push or hold.
- Use: stealth takedown, bodyguard control, non-lethal.
- Camera: over-shoulder from behind.
- Seed: 主角从对手挥拳外侧绕到背后，双手扣住肩膀，把对方向前推离出口。

## Weapons and Props

### 31. Knife Line Evasion
- Grammar: identify blade line, step offline, hand checks weapon arm, counter or escape.
- Use: high danger, short weapon realism.
- Camera: knife passes foreground, body reaction clear.
- Seed: 刀尖从右侧刺来，主角后撤半步并向外侧移线，左手压住持刀前臂。

### 32. Knife Disarm Control
- Grammar: avoid blade, two-on-one wrist control, strike forearm/hand, weapon drops.
- Use: professional action, non-gory disarm.
- Camera: close hand insert plus medium.
- Seed: 主角双手夹住持刀手腕，转动拇指线并用肩撞打乱重心，刀柄松开。

### 33. Baton / Short Stick Strike
- Grammar: guard, forehand/backhand strike, block, wrist recovery.
- Use: police, security, street fight.
- Camera: medium, stick arc visible.
- Seed: 主角右手持短棍横扫对方前臂，立刻反手下压挡开第二次抓取。

### 34. Staff Long-Range Control
- Grammar: two-hand grip, sweep/poke/block, distance maintained, reset guard.
- Use: warehouse, dojo, wuxia-grounded action.
- Camera: medium wide, preserve full weapon.
- Seed: 主角双手横握长棍，左扫逼退一人，棍尾点刺第二人胸前位置，再收回身前。

### 35. Sword Clash / Blade Bind
- Grammar: blade meets blade, pressure bind, foot pivot, disengage or riposte.
- Use: wuxia, period duel, fantasy grounded.
- Camera: medium, avoid only close sparks.
- Seed: 两剑相交后主角向前压刃，右脚绕半步改变角度，剑尖从下方重新刺出。

### 36. Improvised Chair Fight
- Grammar: chair as barrier, push/block/trip, opponent interrupted.
- Use: comedy, bar, classroom, survival.
- Camera: medium to show prop relation.
- Seed: 主角把椅子推到两人之间，对方被椅背卡住，主角绕侧面脱身。

### 37. Bottle / Small Object Distraction
- Grammar: object toss or smash near not into face, flinch, escape/counter.
- Use: bar, kitchen, desperate distraction.
- Camera: insert object path then reaction.
- Seed: 主角把空瓶扔向对方脚边制造响声，对方低头一瞬，主角侧身冲过。

### 38. Shield / Backpack Guard
- Grammar: soft object raised, absorbs hit, push creates space.
- Use: student, civilian, chase, non-martial hero.
- Camera: medium, show object deformation.
- Seed: 主角把背包举到胸前挡住棍击，借冲击向后滑步，再推开对方。

### 39. Gun Retention / Close-Quarters Redirect
- Grammar: weapon already close, hand redirects muzzle away, body angle changes, control wrist.
- Use: thriller, tactical close action. Avoid firing unless requested.
- Camera: close but safe, muzzle direction clear.
- Seed: 对方抬枪逼近，主角左手压开枪口方向，身体侧移离开枪线，右手扣住手腕。

### 40. Rope / Cloth Entangle
- Grammar: cloth wraps wrist/weapon, pull off-balance, step around.
- Use: stylized, martial arts, costume-driven fight.
- Camera: medium wide, show cloth line.
- Seed: 主角甩出围巾缠住对方手腕，向侧后方一拉，对方肩膀被带偏。

## Space, Group, Chase, and Style

### 41. Hallway One-vs-Many
- Grammar: enemies enter one at a time, protagonist redirects each into walls/doors.
- Use: corridor pressure, action climax.
- Camera: 35mm tracking, stable axis.
- Seed: 第一人正面冲来被肘击逼退，第二人从左侧抓肩被推撞墙，第三人刚进入画面。

### 42. Doorway Bottleneck Fight
- Grammar: narrow threshold controls number of attackers, shove, jam, slam door.
- Use: apartment, hotel, safe-room breach.
- Camera: static or slight push, doorway frames action.
- Seed: 主角站在门内侧，利用门框挡住第二个人，只把第一人手臂拉进来反制。

### 43. Staircase Fight
- Grammar: vertical imbalance, rail grab, step trip, controlled fall or shove.
- Use: danger and height, chase transition.
- Camera: low/upstairs angle, preserve orientation.
- Seed: 主角抓住扶手稳住身体，右脚踢开对方踩上的台阶，让对方跌坐在楼梯上。

### 44. Elevator Fight
- Grammar: tiny space, elbows, shoulder check, wall pin, button/door beat.
- Use: sudden violence, compressed tension.
- Camera: wide-ish 24/35mm, avoid unreadable close chaos.
- Seed: 电梯门刚关，主角侧身避开抓手，右肘短促击中，对方后背撞到电梯壁。

### 45. Car Interior Fight
- Grammar: seat belt/steering/door constraints, wrist fight, head duck, elbow, door push.
- Use: thriller, chase, cramped realism.
- Camera: dashboard/side window angle, tight but clear.
- Seed: 主角坐在副驾低头躲开挥来的手臂，右手抓住对方手腕压向中控台。

### 46. Parkour Chase Contact
- Grammar: run, vault, dodge grab, slide/rail turn, preserve direction.
- Use: urban chase, youth energy, escape.
- Camera: wide side/rear tracking, foreground speed.
- Seed: 主角翻过矮墙落地，侧身避开追赶者抓手，滑过管道后继续向右奔跑。

### 47. Crowd Breakthrough
- Grammar: shoulder through crowd, hand protects face/prop, small collisions, exit opens.
- Use: market chase, nightclub, station.
- Camera: handheld behind subject, foreground bodies pass lens.
- Seed: 主角左手护住胸前文件，右肩从人群缝隙挤过，撞开一名阻挡者继续前冲。

### 48. Wuxia Lightness / Wire-Style Exchange
- Grammar: visible foot impulse, glide/leap, weapon/body exchange, controlled landing.
- Use: poetic martial arts, fantasy, elegant duel.
- Camera: smooth lateral or crane-like move, full body.
- Seed: 主角脚尖点地滑步前进，剑尖上挑被格挡后旋身半圈，轻落回防。

### 49. Mocap Game Combo
- Grammar: readable start pose, attack 1, attack 2, impact pose, recovery frame.
- Use: game PV, virtual character, action reference.
- Camera: three-quarter full-body, clean background.
- Seed: 角色从低位防守起势，横扫、反手下劈、肘击三连，最后后撤回到防守姿态。

### 50. Comedy Prop Fight
- Grammar: attack fails, prop interrupts, pause, accidental result, reaction.
- Use: comedic action, ad, family/kitchen/school scene.
- Camera: medium static or gentle follow, show prop and face.
- Seed: 对手挥扫帚扑来，主角用锅盖挡住，清脆一响后两人同时停顿半拍。

## Combining Classes

Use combinations only when they share a clear cause-and-effect path:

- 31 Knife Line Evasion + 19 Wrist Control: knife threat into professional disarm.
- 41 Hallway One-vs-Many + 08 Shoulder Check: corridor pressure with wall impacts.
- 46 Parkour Chase Contact + 17 Knee Slide: chase beat with low dodge.
- 28 Ground Guard Scramble + 29 Mount Escape: survival ground fight.
- 48 Wuxia Lightness + 35 Sword Clash: elegant weapon duel.

Avoid combinations that break tone or physics, such as comedy prop fight plus brutal tactical gun retention, or wuxia lightness plus gritty ground survival, unless the user explicitly wants tonal contrast.
