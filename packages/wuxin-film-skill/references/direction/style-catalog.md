# Cinematic Style Catalog

Use this catalog as a compact visual vocabulary. Adapt the examples instead of copying them blindly. Always preserve the user's subject, product, setting, and required constraints.

## Naturalistic Realism

Use for drama, documentary-like ads, education/consulting scenes, and grounded character work.

- Light: window light, practical lamps, soft overcast, motivated interior light.
- Palette: warm gray, muted beige, soft green, low saturation.
- Camera: 35mm or 50mm, eye-level, stable or slight handheld breathing.
- Materials: natural skin texture, cotton wrinkles, paper grain, wood scratches, imperfect walls.

Example prompt:
`一位30岁亚洲女性坐在普通办公室窗边翻看纸质资料，35mm平视中景，固定机位轻微呼吸感，下午自然窗光从左侧进入，右侧轻微负补光，肤色真实不过度磨皮，棉质衬衫有自然褶皱，木桌有细小划痕和纸张纹理，低饱和暖灰色调，真实剧情片质感。`

## Handheld Documentary

Use for live events, street observation, startup scenes, consulting/education现场感, behind-the-scenes work.

- Light: available light, fluorescent tubes, window spill, mixed color temperature.
- Palette: uncontrolled but believable, slightly muted.
- Camera: 35mm or 50mm handheld follow, micro-shake, imperfect framing.
- Materials: glass reflections, tile floors, plastic chairs, paper folders, jacket fabric.

Example prompt:
`一位教育咨询师在狭长走廊里边走边整理文件，50mm跟拍中近景，轻微手持晃动，走廊顶部冷白荧光灯，玻璃门有微弱反射，地砖带一点湿润反光，深色羊毛混纺外套有真实布料纹理，画面有轻微颗粒，纪录片现场感。`

## Clean Premium Commercial

Use for product ads, course brand films, software/tool commercials, clean institutional brands.

- Light: large softbox, controlled rim light, bounce fill, clean reflection.
- Palette: white, warm white, silver gray, black glass, one accent color.
- Camera: 70-100mm product insert, 35mm human action, stable push-in.
- Materials: brushed metal, black glass, matte rubber, stone, ceramic, clean paper.

Example prompt:
`一只手将银色金属设备轻轻放在浅灰色石材桌面上，85mm微距特写，稳定推近，顶部大面积柔光，边缘有细窄轮廓光，金属倒角出现克制高光，玻璃面板反射干净，石材桌面有细腻哑光纹理，白灰黑极简配色，高级商业片质感。`

## Outdoor Tech Product Hero Shot

Use for commercial product KV, tech product ads, ecommerce hero images, website hero banners, social-media product covers, and AI video product establishing shots where a compact tech or outdoor product needs to feel premium, functional, portable, and brand-owned. This is a product-ad style card, not a general cinematic outdoor style.

- Trigger phrases: 户外科技产品 Hero Shot, 产品广告, 品牌 KV, 电商首屏, 官网 Hero Banner, 科技产品海报, 户外产品摄影, 商业产品片, 产品定场镜头, 露营科技产品, 便携影音设备.
- Do not trigger for: 普通电影感, 人物剧情片, 情绪短片, 自然风格, 户外场景, 科技感, 露营氛围, unless the user clearly asks for a product ad / product KV / commercial product hero frame.
- Visual grammar: product as the cleanest and most legible object in the frame; natural or architectural material base; restrained low-saturation environment; controlled reflections; clear product silhouette; negative space for campaign copy or post typography.
- Product grammar: preserve stable geometry, lens / buttons / speaker grille / ports / handle / texture details; show one functional clue such as projection lens, water drops, charging port, grip texture, speaker mesh, screen glow, or status light.
- Environment grammar: stone block, moss or grass, wet rock, concrete plinth, dark wood, sand, camping ground, matte metal platform, or minimal studio terrain. The base must imply function, durability, portability, or lifestyle instead of acting as random decoration.
- Light: cinematic side-back light, soft ambient fill, controlled rim light around product edges, small specular highlight on glass or metal, natural shadow contact under the product; avoid flat beauty light and random neon.
- Palette: silver gray, graphite black, deep blue gray, stone white, moss green, olive, sand brown, muted warm accent. Keep 2-4 dominant colors and avoid candy saturation.
- Camera: low-angle centered hero composition or 45-degree product angle; 70-100mm for premium product inserts; macro inserts for lens, grille, water drops, buttons, texture; leave top or side negative space when the image is meant for KV.
- Materials: brushed metal, matte plastic, black glass, lens coating, rubber grip, speaker perforation, stone grain, wet moss, grass tips, dust, water droplets, clean bevel highlights.
- Video add-on: 4-6 second locked-off or slow push-in product hero shot; grass / mist / water droplets may move subtly while the product shape stays stable; optional rack focus from foreground texture to the product lens or logo area; no unnecessary orbit unless requested.
- Avoid: making every tech product an outdoor grass-and-rock poster, product hidden by scenery, fake luxury props, wedding-script typography, unreadable generated text, warped logo, product geometry drift, cheap plastic, oversaturated grass, excessive fog, cyberpunk neon, background more attractive than the product.

Example prompt:
`户外科技产品 Hero Shot，主体是一台银灰色便携式智能投影仪，黑色镜头区域、圆形镜头镀膜、正面扬声器网孔和侧面按键清晰可见，产品被放置在一块白灰色粗糙岩石基座上，周围是低矮湿润的深绿色草地，背景为深蓝灰色雾化天空和极简远景。低机位居中英雄构图，镜头略低于产品中线，产品占画面中央，下方岩石形成稳定基座，上方保留干净留白用于后期文案。傍晚电影感侧逆光从右后方打出产品轮廓，左侧有柔和环境补光，镜头玻璃出现克制高光，机身边缘、扬声器孔洞、岩石纹理和草尖水珠都保持清晰。整体色彩控制在银灰、石墨黑、深蓝灰、岩石白灰和苔藓绿之间，高级、克制、便携户外影音品牌广告质感，适合官网 Hero Banner、电商详情页首屏和商业广告片产品定场镜头。`

Negative prompt:
`避免廉价塑料感、产品变形、镜头结构错误、Logo或文字乱码、产品像贴图悬浮、草地过度鲜艳、背景杂乱、过度霓虹、过度雾化、岩石和产品光向不一致、道具抢主体、看不清功能结构、婚礼手写字体、库存海报感。`

## Neo-Noir

Use for suspense, urban pressure, psychological tension, crime, hidden commercial conflict.

- Light: hard side light, neon edge light, practical signs, window blinds, heavy negative fill.
- Palette: deep black, cyan, red, sodium yellow, wet silver.
- Camera: 50-70mm, low angle, slow push, telephoto compression.
- Materials: wet asphalt, glass, leather, rain, smoke, metal.

Example prompt:
`一名穿黑色风衣的男人站在雨夜街角，侧脸被霓虹招牌切出红蓝边光，70mm中近景，低机位缓慢推近，湿柏油路面反射霓虹，皮革手套有冷光高光，玻璃橱窗带雨滴和模糊倒影，强阴影，高反差neo-noir风格。`

## Cyberpunk

Use for near-future cities, technology pressure, data overload, high-tech low-life worlds.

- Light: LED signage, screen glow, neon spill, wet reflections.
- Palette: cyan, magenta, acid green, black, chrome, dirty concrete.
- Camera: 24-35mm street movement, handheld or tracking, layered foreground.
- Materials: wet metal, PVC, cables, holographic screens, rain, vapor, worn posters.

Example prompt:
`未来城市小巷里，一位年轻快递员穿透明PVC雨衣穿过密集霓虹广告牌，24mm广角跟拍，地面湿滑反射蓝紫和酸绿色灯光，墙面有金属管线和旧海报残胶，雨衣边缘出现透明塑料折射，空气里有细雾和水汽，拥挤但主体清楚，赛博朋克电影质感。`

## Vintage 1970s Film

Use for memory, family, old business stories, period atmosphere, nostalgic narration.

- Light: tungsten lamps, soft window spill, warm practicals.
- Palette: warm brown, olive green, faded yellow, muted red.
- Camera: 35-50mm, slight zoom, film grain, soft contrast.
- Materials: wood, old wallpaper, cotton, glass cups, paper ledgers, brass.

Example prompt:
`1970年代小餐馆里，一位父亲把账本推到桌子中央，40mm中景，轻微变焦感，钨丝灯暖黄光，木桌油亮但有旧划痕，墙纸泛黄，玻璃杯边缘有柔和高光，画面带35mm胶片颗粒和轻微暗角，暖棕绿色调，复古剧情片质感。`

## VHS Home Video

Use for found footage, childhood memory, retro horror, family archive, 1990s atmosphere.

- Light: TV glow, indoor lamps, unstable exposure.
- Palette: washed-out color, color bleed, blue screen glow.
- Camera: fixed camcorder, wide angle, slight shake.
- Materials: CRT screen, fabric sofa, plastic remote, carpet, old curtains.

Example prompt:
`一群孩子在90年代客厅里围着电视机玩游戏，固定广角家庭录像机视角，画面轻微抖动，VHS扫描线和色彩溢出，电视蓝光照在脸上，布艺沙发有旧绒毛质感，塑料遥控器反光廉价，角落钨丝灯闪烁，怀旧家庭录像质感。`

## Gothic Dark Fantasy

Use for fate, religion, castles, ritual, dark romance, tragic fantasy.

- Light: candlelight, moonlight, cold backlight, strong shadow.
- Palette: cold gray, black, deep red, tarnished silver.
- Camera: 35-70mm, symmetrical or low angle, slow movement.
- Materials: wet stone, iron, velvet, wax, old silver, dust.

Example prompt:
`一位穿深红天鹅绒长裙的女人站在哥特教堂长廊尽头，50mm对称构图，缓慢前推，烛光从两侧跳动，冷灰石墙带潮湿纹理，铁艺门有黑色氧化痕迹，天鹅绒吸光厚重，地面有微弱水汽反射，暗红与冷灰配色，哥特电影美术风格。`

## Magical Realism

Use for poetic advertising, memory, education themes, quiet wonder, dreams inside real life.

- Light: real-world light plus subtle impossible glow.
- Palette: grounded neutrals with one gentle supernatural accent.
- Camera: 35-50mm, slow push, steady observation.
- Materials: keep real objects tactile, make the magical element restrained.

Example prompt:
`清晨的普通教室里，粉笔灰缓慢漂浮成一条发光的鱼，学生安静看着黑板，35mm中景，窗外柔和晨光，木课桌有铅笔划痕，黑板粉尘真实，漂浮光鱼半透明且光线轻微照亮周围粉尘，低饱和蓝白色调，魔幻现实主义电影质感。`

## Expressionist Psychological

Use for anxiety, inner collapse, nightmare, distorted perception, mental pressure.

- Light: hard top light, exaggerated shadows, unnatural color.
- Palette: green, sick yellow, black, deep red, cold blue.
- Camera: 18-28mm wide angle, low angle, distorted perspective.
- Materials: rough wall paint, long shadows, warped floor lines, harsh surfaces.

Example prompt:
`一位男人坐在狭小房间中央，墙面像向内倾斜压迫他，18mm广角低机位，硬质顶光制造巨大阴影，绿色墙漆带粗糙刷痕，木地板纹理被广角拉长，人物脸部半明半暗，德国表现主义电影感，焦虑、压迫、非现实空间。`

## Ink-Wash Eastern Cinematic

Use for Chinese poetic images, wuxia restraint, mountains, memory, philosophical themes.

- Light: diffused sky light, misty backlight, soft contrast.
- Palette: ink black, paper white, celadon gray, moss green.
- Camera: 70mm long lens for layered landscape, static wide shots, slow drift.
- Materials: mist, wet stone, linen, bamboo, water, paper grain.

Example prompt:
`一位白衣剑客站在山间石桥上，远处云雾缓慢流动，70mm长焦压缩空间，画面大量留白，青灰色山石带湿润纹理，白色麻布衣有细小纤维感，水面反射极淡，整体像宣纸上的淡墨晕染，但人物保持电影写实比例，新中式水墨影像风格。`

## Japanese Quiet Life

Use for daily life, youth, family distance, restrained emotion, quiet relationships.

- Light: soft window light, overcast daylight, clean interior bounce.
- Palette: pale wood, white, soft blue, faded green, low contrast.
- Camera: 35mm locked-off or gentle pan, eye-level observation.
- Materials: pale wood, ceramic, cotton, paper, glass, clean kitchen surfaces.

Example prompt:
`午后的厨房里，一位年轻女孩把洗好的白瓷碗放到木架上，35mm固定中景，窗外柔和自然光，浅木台面有细腻纹路，白瓷碗边缘有柔和高光，棉质围裙有自然褶皱，空气明亮安静，低对比、低饱和、日系生活电影感。`

## French New Wave

Use for young creators, city walking, free dialogue, casual rebellion, light romance.

- Light: natural street light, changing sun patches, available interiors.
- Palette: black and white or muted street color.
- Camera: 28-35mm handheld, jump-cut friendly, imperfect framing.
- Materials: coat fabric, street stone, old books, cigarette ash, cafe glass.

Example prompt:
`巴黎风格街头，一位年轻女人边走边回头说话，手里夹着旧书，28mm手持跟拍，阳光从建筑间断续落在脸上，黑白胶片质感，街道路面石板纹理清楚，风衣布料随风摆动，构图略微随意，有法式新浪潮的即兴感。`

Use for alternate history, retro engineering, mechanical fantasy, Victorian technology.

- Light: warm industrial lamps, furnace glow, dusty shafts.
- Palette: brass, brown leather, dark wood, amber, smoke gray.
- Camera: 35mm workshop scenes, macro inserts for gauges/gears.
- Materials: brass oxidation, leather wear, rivets, steam, glass gauges, old wood.

Use for handmade humor, warmth, children's stories, playful brand films, craft-like explainers.

- Light: small practical lamps, soft tabletop light.
- Palette: warm, tactile, slightly imperfect.
- Camera: macro or close tabletop framing, fixed or small incremental movement.
- Materials: clay fingerprints, paper-card sets, miniature props, hand-cut edges.

## Xianxia / Guofeng Environment Style Cards
### 写实CG修仙PV

Use for grounded, film-like cultivation scenes with believable terrain, real materials, and restrained spiritual elements.

- Trigger phrases: 写实CG修仙, 凡修质感, 真实材质修仙PV, 克制灵气, 动画电影修仙场景.
- Visual grammar: real mountain paths, stone steps, wind-worn cliffs, heavy clouds, practical architecture, believable cloth and stone; magic is present but not decorative.
- Light: overcast skylight, side backlight through clouds, cold mist, restrained god rays.
- Palette: blue gray, wet stone gray, faded green, cold white, small warm highlights.
- Camera: 24-35mm wide establishing shot or 50-70mm layered telephoto; keep figures small when the scene needs scale.
- Materials: wet stone, old wood, linen robes, moss, cloud vapor, mountain dust.
- Avoid: glowing UI-like magic circles, flat game screenshot, anime skin, over-saturated fantasy colors, too many floating particles.

Example prompt:
`写实CG修仙PV大场景，清晨云雾压在群山之间，一座古老宗门石阶沿峭壁向上延伸，几名修士只是画面中的小比例人物，24mm远景广角，低机位看见山体纵深和云层高度，冷灰天光从云缝里透出，石阶潮湿有苔痕，木制山门有风化裂纹，衣袍布料被山风吹动，灵气只以极淡白雾贴着地面流动，真实材质、克制特效、动画电影级写实CG质感。`

### 仙侠云海奇观PV

Use for majestic PV shots where the core appeal is scale: cloud sea, impossible mountain formations, giant sect architecture, flying mounts, and far-distance movement.

- Trigger phrases: 修仙大PV, 仙侠云海奇观, 巨塔仙门, 云海仙门, 仙禽飞行, 宏大仙侠开场.
- Visual grammar: foreground cliff or ancient pine, middle-ground cloud sea and flying creature, background giant tower / sect gate hidden in mist; clear front-middle-back depth.
- Light: sunset or sunrise backlight, large cloud shadows, god rays cutting through mist.
- Palette: warm gold near the sun, blue gray clouds, green-black cliffs, pale white mist.
- Camera: ultra-wide establishing shot, high vantage point, 21-28mm, characters are small silhouettes; use atmospheric perspective to show distance.
- Materials: cloud vapor, cliff stone, pine needles, bronze / dark wood architecture, feather or crane texture.
- Avoid: close-up hero posing, flat mountain climbing scene, small tourist landscape, empty fog without architectural anchor.

Example prompt:
`仙侠云海奇观PV开场，极远景俯瞰群峰漂浮在云海之上，左侧前景是一棵悬崖古松和陡峭岩壁，中景一只巨大仙鹤横穿云层，背上站着三名修士，远处一座高耸入云的仙门巨塔从山体中升起，塔身半隐在云雾里，21mm超广角大远景，夕阳从左侧云层后透出金色神光，蓝灰云海、青绿山体、暖金边缘光，羽毛、湿岩、松针和古建筑木石细节清晰，宏大、飘渺、游戏PV级东方幻想场景。`

### 国风幻想厚涂仙界场景

Use for painterly celestial-realm environments: golden sky, floating platforms, circular plazas, palace clusters, ink-wash edges, and concept-art composition. This is not realistic CG; keep the painterly guofeng feeling.

- Trigger phrases: 国风幻想厚涂, 仙界主城, 金色天穹仙宗, 云端仙宗平台, 水墨厚涂修仙场景, 东方幻想场景概念图.
- Visual grammar: floating mountain islands, circular jade platforms, palace roofs, bridges, distant silhouettes, birds or spirit beasts for scale; architecture should feel designed, not random.
- Light: broad golden celestial sky, mist diffusion, soft rim light around platforms, luminous halos or distant sacred rings.
- Palette: pale gold, celadon green, ink gray, jade white, soft yellow haze.
- Camera: high-angle panoramic establishing shot, 24-35mm, layered from foreground balcony to middle palace cluster to distant mountains.
- Materials: jade stone, carved railings, glazed tiles, mist bands, ink-wash cliff edges, hand-painted cloud texture.
- Avoid: photorealistic hard-surface CG, Western fantasy castle, neon cyber magic, over-detailed 3D render, plastic gold.

Example prompt:
`国风幻想厚涂仙界场景，金色天穹下漂浮着一座云端仙宗主城，前景是半截古亭栏杆和松枝剪影，中景由多个圆形玉石平台、拱桥和层叠宫殿组成，远景是淡墨色悬浮山峰和穿云而过的巨大灵兽剪影，天空有柔和圆形圣光但不做科幻UI，35mm高角度全景构图，淡金、青绿、玉白、墨灰色彩，云雾像水墨晕染，建筑边缘保留厚涂笔触和概念设定稿质感，宏大、空灵、东方幻想游戏场景原画。`

### 凡修冷峻宗门

Use for austere, lower-magic cultivation worlds where the appeal is discipline, hardship, sect hierarchy, and mountain realism rather than celestial spectacle.

- Trigger phrases: 凡修宗门, 冷峻修仙, 低魔修仙, 山门试炼, 修仙宗门写实场景.
- Visual grammar: steep stone stairs, severe mountain gate, gray robes, wind, banners, sparse architecture, long human procession; scale comes from height and distance.
- Light: cold dawn, cloud-covered sky, thin side light, hard mountain wind.
- Palette: slate gray, cold blue, dark pine green, off-white cloth, weathered brown wood.
- Camera: long-lens compression from below the stairs or wide establishing shot with tiny figures; avoid heroic close-ups unless requested.
- Materials: cracked stone, worn wooden gates, hemp robes, iron bells, fog, pine bark.
- Avoid: excessive glowing magic, fairyland softness, golden heavenly palette, decorative palace fantasy, over-romantic posing.

Example prompt:
`凡修冷峻宗门大场景，阴冷清晨的高山石阶从画面下方一直延伸到云雾中的巨大山门，数十名灰袍弟子沿阶而上，人物比例很小，70mm长焦压缩山体纵深，远处山门木梁风化开裂，铁铃和旧旗在冷风里晃动，天空是低压蓝灰云层，石阶湿冷、苔痕明显，松树黑绿色压在两侧，几乎没有可见法术，只保留薄雾和肃杀气，克制、真实、冷峻的修仙宗门PV质感。`


## Common Style Negative Prompts

Use or adapt these when needed:

`避免廉价塑料质感、蜡像皮肤、过度磨皮、网红妆、随机霓虹、过饱和、背景杂乱、不可读文字、漂浮UI、产品形状漂移、额外手指、手腕扭曲、突然跳切、镜头无故旋转、运动模糊遮挡主体、低清晰度、AI感CG、库存照片笑容。`
