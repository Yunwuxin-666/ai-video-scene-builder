# Narrative gates

Run these gates before camera, lighting, style, VFX, or platform compilation.

## Causality gate

For every beat, verify:

- a visible cause exists;
- the protagonist has a readable goal;
- the action changes the state;
- the result creates or enables the next beat;
- the ending answers the opening problem.

Remove or justify a beat that can disappear without changing the story.

## Ambiguity gate

- Preserve explicit facts.
- Infer no more than three material creative facts.
- Do not infer identity, brand claims, premise, or ending when they change direction.
- Ask one question only for a directional unknown.
- Prefer a simple, coherent route over combining several plausible routes.

## Density gate

Each generation unit must have:

- one narrative function;
- one central visible state change;
- one primary subject action;
- no more than three timed sub-beats unless the target platform and shot design support a longer continuous take.

Split the unit when subject, location, time, visual law, or action objective changes sharply.

## Route gate

Choose one active route. Keep alternatives separate. Never merge the best-looking pieces of several routes without a new coherence pass.

## Pass condition

Do not advance to visual design until:

- every beat has cause, action, and result;
- the ending state is explicit;
- unresolved items cannot silently change the premise;
- the active route is singular.
